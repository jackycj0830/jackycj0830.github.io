By utilizing knowledge distillation from top-tier models to generate advanced prompts, I guide and simulate local models to produce output comparable to cloud-native giants, maximizing performance under constrained hardware resources.

Core Methodology: Applying Knowledge Distillation

In both system operations and AI development, I apply the concept of "Knowledge Distillation" as a framework for solving complex challenges:

  • Target Distillation (Multi-dimensional Impact): Moving beyond basic technical delivery to uncover hidden business value. For example, when leading global IT infrastructure upgrades, I integrated M365 Copilot and automation workflows to optimize server inventory and migration efficiency.
  • Feature Distillation (Logical Reasoning Chain): Tackling critical security vulnerabilities (e.g., zero-days) by establishing a clear "Discover-Analyze-Act" reasoning chain. This involves identifying anomalies via log analysis, evaluating cross-platform dependencies, and executing automated remediation scripts to ensure zero downtime.
  • Local Student Model (Lightweight AI Tooling): Treating massive cloud LLMs as "Teacher Models" to extract advanced logic frameworks, and "distilling" them into lightweight, locally-developed Python tools. This self-built AI pipeline significantly boosts daily operational efficiency and demonstrates the practical deployment of AI in the enterprise.
